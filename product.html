Shipping & Returns

International orders typically arrive within 7-14 business days. Express shipping options are available at checkout for select destinations. We accept returns within 30 days of purchase for unopened items in original packaging. For opened fragrances, please contact our customer care team.

How long do the fragrances last on skin?Our Eau de Parfum formulations typically last 8-12 hours on skin, though longevity varies with skin type, climate, and the specific composition.
