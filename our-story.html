Our Beginning

Born from Emotion.
Crafted with Intention.

Luvior Paris was born from a singular belief: that fragrance is the most intimate form of art. Not something you wear — something you become.

Founded in the heart of Paris, we set out to create scents that do not follow trends but define them. Every composition begins with an emotion, a memory, a story waiting to be told through rare and extraordinary ingredients.

We do not create
just fragrances.

We create
memories,
identities,
and moments.

Creative Director

Alexandre Dumont

“Fragrance is the invisible signature of who you are.”

With over two decades in luxury perfumery, Alexandre founded Luvior Paris to redefine the relationship between scent and identity. His compositions draw from art, architecture, and the raw beauty of nature — always in pursuit of something that feels both timeless and deeply personal.
